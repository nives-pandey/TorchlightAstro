From an expert perspective, the modal-based, step-by-step approach ("Begin Your Cosmic Journey Now ✨") is vastly superior for user engagement, retention, and ultimately, for the success of your app.

Here’s a breakdown of why.

User Engagement & Retention
The modal flow is the clear winner here.

Lower Friction, Higher Speed: A modal feels instant. It keeps the user in the context of the beautiful home page and delivers the "aha!" moment of their chart reveal much faster than a full page load. This speed is critical for a mobile audience with high expectations.

Psychologically Less Intimidating: A single, long form can feel like work. A step-by-step process within a modal (e.g., "First, your birth date...") breaks the task into manageable chunks, which dramatically increases the likelihood that a user will complete it.

Delightful First Impression: A smooth, app-like modal experience is a delightful first interaction. A positive first impression is the single most important factor in whether a user will return. The full-page form feels like a standard, clunky website, which breaks the "sanctuary" feel.

SEO (Search Engine Optimization)
While it seems like a dedicated page (/personal) would be better for SEO, the modal approach is still the smarter strategy.

User Signals are King: Google's primary goal is to rank pages that users love. A dedicated form page (/personal) is likely to have a very high bounce rate (users leaving immediately) and low time-on-page because its only purpose is data entry. These negative user signals would actually harm your SEO ranking over time.

The Home Page is Your SEO Powerhouse: Your SEO strategy should focus on the rich, informative content on your home page. This is the page that will attract users from search engines. The modal's job is to convert that traffic with the best possible experience.

How Many Times Should the CTA Appear?
Placing the call-to-action (CTA) three times is a standard and highly effective design practice.

Top of the Page: For users who are immediately convinced and want to get started.

Middle of the Page: For users who need to scroll and learn more about your features before they are ready to act.

Bottom of the Page: As a final, convenient next step for users who have read everything.

This ensures that a user never has to scroll far to find their next step, which is crucial for conversion.

The Final Recommendation
Consolidate to a single, superior user flow.

Remove the /personal page and the "Begin Your Journey" button that links to it.

Make the "Begin Your Cosmic Journey Now ✨" button your single, primary call-to-action.

Use this same button and its modal functionality in all three locations on your home page.

This will create a single, clear, and psychologically effective path for every user, maximizing engagement and retention from their very first interaction.

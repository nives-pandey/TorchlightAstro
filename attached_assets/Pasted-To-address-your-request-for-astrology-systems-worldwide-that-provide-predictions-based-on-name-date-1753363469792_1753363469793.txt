### Astrology Systems for Predictions

#### 1. Western Astrology
- **Description**: Western astrology, based on the tropical zodiac, uses the positions of celestial bodies (Sun, Moon, planets) at the time of birth to create a natal chart. It emphasizes 12 zodiac signs, houses, and planetary aspects for predictions about personality, relationships, career, and life events. The name is not typically used for predictions but may be relevant for user profiling or numerology integration.
- **Inputs Used**:
  - **DOB**: Determines Sun sign and planetary positions.
  - **Time of Birth**: Essential for calculating the Ascendant (rising sign) and house cusps, which change every two hours.
  - **Place of Birth**: Provides geographic coordinates for accurate chart calculations.
  - **Name**: Optional, used for personalization or numerology (e.g., name-based compatibility).
- **Predictions**:
  - Personality traits (Sun, Moon, Ascendant signs).
  - Life events via transits and progressions.
  - Relationship compatibility through synastry.
  - Annual forecasts (solar return charts).
- **Integration Sources**:
  - **Swiss Ephemeris**: A high-precision ephemeris for planetary calculations, widely used for professional-grade accuracy. Available as a library for integration (C-based, with Node.js bindings via `swisseph`). License required for commercial use. [alabe.com]
  - **Astro.com API**: Provides natal chart calculations and interpretations. Requires API access agreement. [astro.cafeastrology.com]
  - **Open-Source Libraries**: Libraries like `flatlib` (Python) or `astrology.js` (JavaScript) for basic chart calculations.
- **Notes**: Western astrology is the most widely recognized system globally, making it a core component for Torchlight’s freemium model. Swiss Ephemeris is ideal for precise calculations, aligning with Torchlight’s professional-grade focus.

#### 2. Vedic Astrology (Jyotish)
- **Description**: Vedic astrology, or Jyotish, is an ancient Indian system based on the sidereal zodiac. It uses precise birth data to create a Janam Kundli (natal chart) and focuses on 27 Nakshatras (lunar mansions), Dasha periods, and planetary influences for detailed predictions. Name is used for Naam-Karan (name selection) and sometimes numerology.
- **Inputs Used**:
  - **DOB**: Determines planetary positions and Nakshatras.
  - **Time of Birth**: Critical for Lagna (Ascendant) and accurate Dasha calculations.
  - **Place of Birth**: Ensures correct astronomical calculations.
  - **Name**: Used for Naam-Karan or numerology-based predictions.
- **Predictions**:
  - Dasha periods: Time-based cycles predicting life phases.
  - Sade Sati: Saturn’s 7.5-year cycle impacting life events.
  - Nakshatra phal: Detailed personality and destiny insights.
  - Varshphal: Annual predictions based on solar return.
  - Ayurvedic recommendations: Health and lifestyle guidance.
- **Integration Sources**:
  - **Swiss Ephemeris**: Supports sidereal calculations for Vedic charts. [alabe.com]
  - **AstroSage API**: Offers Vedic chart calculations, Nakshatra analysis, and Dasha predictions. [astrosage.com]
  - **Prokerala API**: Provides Vedic birth chart calculations in North and South Indian styles. [prokerala.com]
  - **DrikPanchang**: Offers APIs for Vedic astrology calculations, including Panchang and Muhurat. [drikpanchang.com]
- **Notes**: Vedic astrology’s historical accuracy and depth align with Torchlight’s emphasis on ancient wisdom. AstroSage’s API is a strong candidate for integration due to its comprehensive Vedic features.

#### 3. Chinese Astrology
- **Description**: Chinese astrology is based on a 12-year cycle of animal signs and five elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water). It uses DOB for the animal sign and element but can incorporate time and place for more detailed BaZi (Four Pillars of Destiny) analysis. Name is less critical but may be used for cultural personalization.
- **Inputs Used**:
  - **DOB**: Determines animal sign and element based on the lunar calendar year.
  - **Time of Birth**: Used in BaZi for hour pillar calculations.
  - **Place of Birth**: Optional for BaZi, adjusts for local time.
  - **Name**: May be used for cultural or numerological analysis.
- **Predictions**:
  - Personality traits based on animal signs and elements.
  - Annual and monthly forecasts based on elemental cycles.
  - Compatibility analysis between signs.
  - BaZi: Detailed life path analysis using year, month, day, and hour pillars.
- **Integration Sources**:
  - **Chinese Astrology APIs**: Limited public APIs exist; custom algorithms are often needed for BaZi calculations. Libraries like `chinese-lunar` (JavaScript) convert Gregorian dates to lunar calendar dates.
  - **AstroSeek**: Offers basic Chinese astrology calculations, including BaZi. [astroseek.com]
  - **Custom Implementation**: Use open-source lunar calendar libraries (e.g., `lunar-javascript`) and element compatibility tables to build a BaZi engine.
- **Notes**: Chinese astrology is simpler for basic predictions but requires custom development for BaZi integration. It’s a key component of Torchlight’s multi-system approach, appealing to East Asian markets.
